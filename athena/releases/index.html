What's new in Athena Studio

Athena Studio is in active development. Here's what changed, newest first. Public builds for macOS are coming soon.

0.13v0.13.0 – v0.13.34
Oct 27–28, 2026
  • NewAthena follows up-to-date coding rules: per-layer language and type rules, modern syntax, scaffolds, and a type-check step in verification.
  • ImprovedThe app interface was rebuilt on TypeScript and React for a steadier screen.
  • FixedLarge pull requests are reviewed code-first. If a diff had to be cut short, an approval asks you to confirm.
  • FixedPlans with steps left are no longer completed or archived automatically.
  • Fixed⌘← and ⌘→ move to the start and end of the line, fixing repeated characters with Korean input.
  • FixedThe agent monitor shows in every workspace.
0.12v0.12.0 – v0.12.3
Oct 27, 2026
  • NewPane details show token usage.
  • ImprovedThe agent monitor shows only agents that are working, labeled by repository.
  • FixedBlocked items in pane details are shown in red.
0.11v0.11.0 – v0.11.5
Oct 26–27, 2026
  • NewThe status board became the work board: an issue list as the first screen, finished work archived automatically, and a pane details pop-up.
  • ImprovedPane details are easier to read and have a back button.
  • ImprovedPane title bars are always visible, focused or not.
0.10v0.10.0 – v0.10.7
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewRepositories in the sidebar get two-letter badges.
  • ImprovedTerminals redraw smoothly in real time while you resize the window, without jumping lines or overflow.
  • FixedPull requests opened after a plan is complete are also reviewed and merged automatically.
0.9v0.9.0
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewRepository logos in the sidebar. The Athena workspace always shows the Athena logo.
0.8v0.8.0 – v0.8.2
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewRepository images in expanded sidebar rows.
  • Fixed⌘⌫ deletes to the start of the line, and ⇧↩ inserts a new line instead of sending.
0.7v0.7.0
Oct 26, 2026
  • ImprovedCore updates apply right away, even while agents are working. Commits and heavy commands finish first.
0.6v0.6.0 – v0.6.1
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewAutomatic pull request review starts as soon as work is pushed and carries over earlier approvals. Without Codex, Claude reviews twice.
  • FixedAutomatic flows resolve merge conflicts more reliably.
0.5v0.5.0
Oct 26, 2026
  • ImprovedAutomatic review settles implementation details itself, raises all comments at once and blocks only on real problems.
0.4v0.4.0
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewRename repositories and give them images. Shared through pack export and backup.
0.3v0.3.0 – v0.3.1
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewChoose a model for each role in a pack: agents, pull request review and learning review.
0.2v0.2.0
Oct 26, 2026
  • NewVersion numbers. Every release is tagged and the app shows its version.
