Chagok Privacy Policy

Version 1, posted September 30, 2026, effective September 30, 2026

한국어

Novum Lab (NOVUM-LAB) (representative: Eunju Kim; "we" or "us") maintains this Privacy Policy to protect your personal information and to handle related complaints smoothly in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea ("PIPA").

1. If you do not sign in

While you use the app without signing in, your goals, stickers, notes and photos are stored only on your device. We do not receive these records.

However, each time the app opens it connects to a payment verification company (RevenueCat) to get the prices of sticker packs and ad removal and to check what you have bought, and when an ad is shown, the ad company (Google) collects information directly from your device. The information sent in these cases is described in sections 3 and 4 below. The app has no usage analytics tools.

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive payment method information such as card numbers.

To show item prices, check the packs you have bought and recover them on other devices, we process the following information.

4. Ads

While you use the app for free, ads appear at the bottom of the goal screen, and on the pack screen you can watch an ad to try a pack for a day. Ads no longer appear once you buy ad removal.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. We do not request personalized ads for any user. We do not choose ads by guessing your interests, and we do not send the screens you view in the app or your records to the ad company.
  • To show ads, keep the same ad from appearing too often, prevent fraudulent clicks and count ads, Google directly collects the following information from your device: device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID; this app does not request App Tracking permission on iPhone, so the iPhone advertising identifier is not passed on), IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device type and operating system, records of ads viewed or tapped, and error and performance information.
  • How Google processes this information is described in Google's partner sites policy.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

5. Retention and destruction

  1. When you delete your account, we destroy the account information and records on our servers (section 2) without delay (login records and server operation records follow paragraphs 9 and 10, and deletion processing records follow paragraph 11). Before that, if you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the login token, and if you signed in with Kakao, we ask Kakao to disconnect. If you signed in with Google, the app asks Google to disconnect. If you delete your account in the app, the records stored on that device are deleted too.
  2. If you disconnect Chagok in Kakao or close your Kakao account, we also use the Kakao member number Kakao provides to find your account and destroy the account information and records on our servers in the same way. In this case, records stored on your device are not deleted, so you can delete the app to remove them if needed.
  3. To prepare for server failures, we encrypt and back up the database and photos every day. Backups are kept for 30 days, and backups older than 30 days are deleted in a cleanup run once a week. Deleted information may therefore remain in backups for up to 37 days. During this time, information in backups is not used for anything other than recovery.
  4. Purchase information (section 3) is used for restoring purchases and for keeping transaction records as required by law. Even if you delete your account, RevenueCat's purchase records are kept for the periods below and then destroyed (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 6, and its Enforcement Decree, Article 6). The account identifier in these records belongs to a deleted account, so it no longer links to any Chagok account.
    • Records of contracts, withdrawals and the like: 5 years
    • Records of payment and supply of goods and the like: 5 years
    • Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling (including inquiry emails): 3 years
  5. Information kept as required by law is stored separately from other personal information and is not used for any other purpose (PIPA Article 21(3)).
  6. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.
  7. When you delete a goal, sticker or record in the app, a row marked as deleted stays on our servers so other devices can be kept in sync. The content of that row (goal name, note, photo location) is destroyed along with your account under paragraph 1 when you delete your account.
  8. If destruction fails after you disconnect in Kakao (paragraph 2), we separately keep the Kakao member number and the time and stage of the failure until destruction is retried, and delete them when destruction is complete.
  9. Login records (section 2: account identifier, IP address used to connect, and the times of login, logout and keeping you logged in) and authentication audit records are kept for 3 months to prevent misuse and investigate security incidents, and are then deleted (Enforcement Decree of the Protection of Communications Secrets Act, Article 41(2)). If you delete your account, we remove the email address and name from that account's records, and only the account identifier, times and IP address remain until this period ends.
  10. Server operation records (request path, time, result, the connection string containing app and device information, and the email address, name and IP address included in login requests, among others) are used only to find errors and investigate security incidents, and are deleted after being kept for 3 months. They remain for this period even if you delete your account.
  11. Records that an account deletion was processed (account identifier, request and processing times, request path, result) are kept for 1 year to confirm that the deletion request was handled and to answer inquiries, and are then deleted. These records do not include email addresses or phone numbers.

6. Provision to third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where the law specifically provides otherwise. The ad information in section 4 is not something we receive and pass on; Google collects it directly from your device.

9. Children and minimum age for accounts

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete any such information we hold.

You cannot connect an account if you are under 14, or under the minimum age set by the country where you live if that age is higher. We check that you meet this age requirement when you first connect an account. If you are 13 or older but below it, you can use the app on your device without signing in.

If you stop signing up on the consent screen after logging in, we immediately delete the account information created up to that point. If we learn that an account was created for someone below this age, we delete that account without delay. Regardless of age, we request only non-personalized ads for all users.

10. Your rights

  1. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing.
  2. You can delete your account at any time in the app's Settings tab. If you cannot use the app, follow the account deletion instructions.
  3. To view or get a copy of transaction records such as your purchase history, send a request to the email address in section 13 (Enforcement Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 6(2)).
  4. For any other request, contact the privacy officer below, and we will handle it without delay.

11. Security measures

  • Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS). Backups are stored encrypted.
  • The database applies row-level permissions so that each user can read and write only their own data.
  • Access to the servers and database is limited to the operator and allowed only with authentication keys, and the operator's access records are kept for at least 1 year.

12. Photo and camera permissions

We ask for access to your photo library only when you attach a photo to a record, and for camera access only when you take a photo to keep in a record. Photos you take are treated the same as photos you attach. If you refuse these permissions, you can still use stickers and notes. The app does not ask for any other permissions.
