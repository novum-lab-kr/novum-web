Novum Lab (NOVUM-LAB) (representative: Eunju Kim; "we" or "us") maintains this Privacy Policy to protect your personal information and to handle related complaints smoothly in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (「개인정보 보호법」; "PIPA").

1. If you do not sign in

While you use the app without signing in, your goals, stickers, notes and photos are stored only on your device. We do not receive these records.

However, each time the app opens it connects to a payment verification company (RevenueCat) to get the prices of sticker packs and ad removal and to check what you have bought, and when an ad is shown, the ad company (Google) collects information directly from your device. The information sent in these cases is described in sections 3 and 4 below. From app version 1.1.0, the app sends app error reports, and sends usage records only if you consent (section 16). The usage analytics tool used on the website's front page is separate from the app and is described in section 15.

When you open a Shared Goal invite link, the app or the web invite page gets an invite preview (section 6, paragraph 2) from our server, even if you have not signed in. To stop people from entering random invite codes to find out about other people's goals, for requests with an invite code that does not exist we record the connecting address (IP address) and time, and once 30 such codes are entered from the same place within 1 hour, we stop showing previews from there. These records are used only to prevent misuse and are deleted after 1 hour (section 5, paragraph 15). We process them under PIPA Article 15(1)6.

4. Ads

While you use the app for free, ads appear at the bottom of the goal screen, and on the pack screen you can watch an ad to try a pack for a day. Ads no longer appear once you buy ad removal.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. We do not request personalized ads for any user. We do not choose ads by guessing your interests, and we do not send the screens you view in the app or your records to the ad company.
  • To show ads, keep the same ad from appearing too often, prevent fraudulent clicks and count ads, Google directly collects the following information from your device: device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID; this app does not request App Tracking permission on iPhone, so the iPhone advertising identifier is not passed on), IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device type and operating system, records of ads viewed or tapped, and error and performance information.
  • How Google processes this information is described in Google's partner sites policy.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

5. Retention and destruction

  1. When you delete your account, we destroy the account information and records on our servers (section 2) without delay (login records and server operation records follow paragraphs 9 and 10, deletion processing records follow paragraph 11, and report details follow paragraph 14). Before that, if you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the login token, and if you signed in with Kakao, we ask Kakao to disconnect. If you signed in with Google, the app asks Google to disconnect. If you delete your account in the app, the records stored on that device are deleted too.
  2. If you disconnect Chagok in Kakao or close your Kakao account, we also use the Kakao member number Kakao provides to find your account and destroy the account information and records on our servers in the same way. In this case, records stored on your device are not deleted, so you can delete the app to remove them if needed.
  3. To prepare for server failures, we encrypt and back up the database and photos every day. Backups are kept for 30 days, and backups older than 30 days are deleted in a cleanup run once a week. Deleted information may therefore remain in backups for up to 37 days. During this time, information in backups is not used for anything other than recovery.
  4. Purchase information (section 3) is used for restoring purchases and for keeping transaction records as required by law. Even if you delete your account, RevenueCat's purchase records are kept for the periods below and then destroyed (Article 6 of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (「전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률」) and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree). The account identifier in these records belongs to a deleted account, so it no longer links to any Chagok account.
    • Records of contracts, withdrawals and the like: 5 years
    • Records of payment and supply of goods and the like: 5 years
    • Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling (including inquiry emails): 3 years
  5. Information kept as required by law is stored separately from other personal information and is not used for any other purpose (PIPA Article 21(3)).
  6. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.
  7. When you delete a goal, sticker or record in the app, a row marked as deleted stays on our servers so other devices can be kept in sync. The content of that row (goal name, note, photo location) is destroyed under paragraph 1 when you delete your account.
  8. If destruction fails after you disconnect in Kakao (paragraph 2), we separately keep the Kakao member number and the time and stage of the failure until destruction is retried, and delete them when destruction is complete.
  9. Login records (section 2: account identifier, IP address used to connect, and the times of login, logout and keeping you logged in) and authentication audit records are kept for 3 months to prevent misuse and investigate security incidents, and are then deleted (Article 41(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Protection of Communications Secrets Act (「통신비밀보호법 시행령」)). If you delete your account, we remove the email address and name from that account's records, and only the account identifier, times and IP address remain until this period ends.
  10. Server operation records (request path, time, result, the connection string containing app and device information, and the email address, name and IP address included in login requests, among others) are used only to find errors, investigate security incidents and prevent misuse, and are deleted after being kept for 3 months. They remain for this period even if you delete your account.
  11. Records that an account deletion was processed (account identifier, request and processing times, request path, result) are kept for 1 year to confirm that the deletion request was handled and to answer inquiries, and are then deleted. These records do not include email addresses or phone numbers.
  12. Error reports and usage records (section 16) are deleted when the retention periods in section 16 have passed. Usage records are deleted in a cleanup run once a month, so they may remain for up to one more month after the retention period. Deleting your account does not delete these records immediately, but the account identifier left in them belongs to a deleted account, so it no longer links to any Chagok account. If you want them deleted immediately, send a request to the email address in section 13.
  13. When you leave or are removed from a Shared Goal, we delete the display name and sticker you used in that goal without delay, and keep only the fact and time that you left. Spaces you filled on a goal where Members fill one board together, and their records, are deleted. If it was a goal where each Member fills their own board, your board and records stay as your own goal and are treated as backup information under section 2. When a Shared Goal is deleted, its invite codes and any remaining markers are deleted too. However, so that the goal can also be removed from your other devices on the same account, only the goal identifier and the time you left are kept until you delete your account. The display name and sticker you last used in a Shared Goal and your list of blocked people are deleted when you delete your account, and when you unblock someone, that person's entry is deleted.
  14. Report details (section 2) and report notification emails (section 8) are kept for 1 year from the day handling is complete to deal with disputes and check for repeat cases, and are then deleted. Even if you delete your account, report details remain for this period, and only that account's identifier is deleted.
  15. The connecting address (IP address) and time of invite preview requests with an invite code that does not exist (section 1), and records of attempts to join a Shared Goal by invitation (section 2), are deleted after 1 hour. Expired records are deleted when the next request comes in, so they may remain slightly longer than 1 hour. These requests also remain in the server operation records in paragraph 10 for 3 months.

6. Provision to third parties

  1. We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where the law specifically provides otherwise. The ad information in section 4 is not something we receive and pass on; Google collects it directly from your device. The website usage statistics in section 15 are also collected by Google directly from visitors' browsers.
  2. When you use Shared Goals, the following information is visible to the people you choose. Notes and photos are not visible to anyone. Nothing is visible to users who have blocked each other.
    • Members (people you invited, or people whose invitation you accepted): your display name, the sticker you chose, the spaces you filled on that goal, and the order in which you joined
    • People with the invite link: without signing in, in the app and on the web invite page (addresses starting with https://chagok.novum-lab.com/invite/), they can see the goal name, the board type and number of spaces (for a calendar board, the end date), the display name of the user who created the goal, and the number of Members and the stickers they chose. If the invite link is passed on to someone else, that person can see this too. Once the creator makes a new invite link, the old link can no longer be used to view it.

9. Children under 14

Children under 14 cannot connect an account. We check whether you are 14 or older when you first connect an account, and children under 14 can use the app on their device without signing in.

If you stop signing up on the consent screen after logging in, we immediately delete the account information created up to that point. If we learn that an account was created for a child under 14, we delete that account without delay. Regardless of age, we request only non-personalized ads for all users.

Usage records (section 16) are sent only with consent, so on the screen asking for consent we tell children under 14 to tap [Don't agree] ([동의 안 함]).

10. Rights and duties of users and legal representatives

  1. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing, and you can withdraw consent for processing you consented to (usage records).
  2. You can delete your account at any time in the app's Settings tab. If you cannot use the app, follow the account deletion request instructions. You can turn off usage records and error reports under "Information sent" in the app's settings.
  3. To view or get a copy of transaction records such as your purchase history, send a request to the email address in section 13 (Article 6(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (「전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률 시행령」)).
  4. For any other request, send it to the email address in section 13. We will handle it without delay and tell you the result by email.
  5. A legal representative or a person you have authorized can also exercise these rights on your behalf. In that case, a power of attorney must be sent with the request.
  6. If you request a correction, we do not use that personal information until the correction is complete.
  7. If you live outside the Republic of Korea, you can exercise the rights in this section in the same way. If the law of your country gives you further rights (for example, to receive your data in a portable form, or to complain to the data protection authority in your country), you can also request them at the email address in section 13.
  8. You must protect your own personal information and must not use another person's account or personal information.

11. Security measures

  • Data in transit between the app and our servers, and between the app and the two companies in section 16, is encrypted (TLS). Backups are stored encrypted.
  • The database applies row-level permissions so that each user can read and write only their own data. In Shared Goals, only Members of the same goal can receive the information in section 6, paragraph 2, and it is not given to people who have blocked each other.
  • Access to the servers and database is limited to the operator and allowed only with authentication keys, and the operator's access records are kept for at least 1 year.
  • The people who handle personal information are limited to one person, the operator.

12. Photo and camera permissions

We ask for access to your photo library only when you attach a photo to a record, and for camera access only when you take a photo to keep in a record. Photos you take are treated the same as photos you attach. If you refuse these permissions, you can still use stickers and notes. The app does not ask for any other permissions.
