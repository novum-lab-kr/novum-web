Chagok Account Deletion Request

한국어

Delete in the app

  1. Open the Chagok app and tap the Settings tab at the bottom.
  2. Under Account and Backup, tap Delete Account.
  3. Check what will be deleted, then delete. The records on our servers and the records on this device are deleted together.

If you cannot use the app

If you signed in with Kakao

Please disconnect Chagok in KakaoTalk.

  1. In KakaoTalk, go to More → Manage Profile → Kakao Account.
  2. In the connected services menu, choose Chagok and tap Disconnect.

When you disconnect, your Chagok account and the records backed up on our servers are deleted without delay. The same applies if you close your Kakao account. Records left on your device are removed when you delete the app. Kakao sign-in does not give us an email address, so we cannot find your account by email.

If you signed in with Apple or Google

Please send the following to chagok.app.help@gmail.com. After checking, we will delete your account without delay and let you know the result.

  • Subject: Chagok account deletion request
  • The sign-in method you used (Apple or Google) and the email address of that account

What is deleted

  • Account information and consent history
  • Goals, stickers, notes and photos backed up on our servers

Backups made to prepare for server failures keep this information for up to 37 days after deletion, and it is then removed automatically. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
