Frequently asked

  • I reinstalled the app and my sticker packs are gone. Sign in to the store account you bought them with, then tap [Restore purchases] in the Settings tab.
  • I want a refund. The store takes the payment, so refunds are requested from the store too. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the order history in the Google Play app.
  • I want to keep my records when I change phones. Connect an account in the Settings tab, then log in with the same account on your new phone to bring your records over.
